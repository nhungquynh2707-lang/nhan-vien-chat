Privacy Policy

Applies to the automated chat assistant of the Sâu Sấy Canxi BBF Facebook Page on Messenger. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

1. Data we collect

When you message the Page, the assistant receives your Page-scoped Messenger ID, your message text and images you send. If you place an order, we also store your phone number, delivery address and the products ordered. We do not ask for or store your name, passwords or payment details.

2. How we use it

Only to answer product questions, quote prices, take and deliver orders, and remember your delivery details for faster repeat orders. We do not use your data for advertising and we never sell it.

3. Sharing

Message content is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate replies. Order details are shared with the shipping carrier to deliver your order. No other sharing.

4. Retention

Recent conversation history is kept for up to 24 hours. Delivery details of customers who ordered are kept for future orders until you ask us to delete them.
